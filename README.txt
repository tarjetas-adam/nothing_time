NOTHING TIME — install as an app on your phone
================================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
- nothing-time.html   -> the app itself (open this)
- icon-180.png        -> app icon (iOS home screen size)
- icon-192.png        -> app icon (Android home screen size)
- icon-512.png        -> app icon (large / splash size)

The icon is already built into nothing-time.html, so you do NOT need
to attach the PNGs manually — they're just included here in case you
ever want the logo on its own.


HOW TO INSTALL
================================================

You can't "download and install" this like a normal app store app —
there's no app store listing. Instead, you open the HTML file in your
phone's browser and add it to your home screen. Once added, it opens
full-screen with its own icon, just like a real app.

iPhone (Safari):
  1. Send nothing-time.html to your phone (AirDrop, email, iCloud
     Drive, Files app — any way you'd normally get a file onto your
     phone) or open the Claude.ai link on your phone directly.
  2. Open it in Safari (not Chrome, not an in-app browser — it must
     be Safari for "Add to Home Screen" to work).
  3. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow pointing up).
  4. Tap "Add to Home Screen".
  5. Tap "Add" in the top right.
  6. "Nothing Time" now sits on your home screen with its icon, and
     opens full-screen, no browser bar.

Android (Chrome):
  1. Get nothing-time.html onto your phone the same way.
  2. Open it in Chrome.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  4. Tap "Add to Home screen" (some phones show "Install app"
     instead — either works).
  5. Confirm the name and tap "Add".
  6. It now sits on your home screen with its icon.


A FEW HONEST NOTES
================================================
- Your marked days are stored inside that browser/app instance only.
  There's no account and no cloud sync, so if you clear site data or
  reinstall, your history could reset.
- If Android doesn't show the custom icon right away, try again after
  the page has fully loaded once in Chrome first.
- This is a personal prototype, not a published app — nothing here
  is submitted to the App Store or Play Store.
